A modern, responsive merchant web dashboard for the TipsyTheoryy SaaS platform. It serves as an operational control center for store owners to manage orders, catalog inventory, marketing campaigns, rider payouts, platform billing, and store performance analytics.

---

## 🛠️ Key Modules & Capabilities

* **📊 Dashboard & Business Analytics:** Real-time operational metrics, store reports, and detailed sales/performance analysis.
* **📦 Order & Product Management:** Live order lifecycle tracking, inventory updates, and complete product management.
* **📢 Marketing & Promotions:** Targeted marketing blast notifications to app users, active promotions, and custom discount rules.
* **🚴 Rider Payouts & Logistics:** Delivery rider payout processing, logistics workflows, and status tracking.
* **💳 Billing & Subscriptions:** SaaS subscription plan management, merchant platform billing, and payment histories.
* **⚙️ Store Management & Settings:** Comprehensive store settings, branding configuration, and business operational rules.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack

* **Frontend Framework:** React.js + Vite
* **Styling & UI:** Tailwind CSS, PostCSS
* **Code Quality & Linting:** ESLint
